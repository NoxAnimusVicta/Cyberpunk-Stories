# Public source directory

This directory links to publisher resources only. It contains no campaign forecast or price catalogue. Detailed evidence, continuity decisions, economic assumptions and spoiler research remain in private local narrator records.

- [R. Talsorian Games downloads](https://rtalsoriangames.com/downloadable-content/): official tabletop supplements; editions and eras differ.
- [Cyberpunk official site](https://www.cyberpunk.net/): games and related media. The site may contain spoilers; opening it is not necessary for play.

Research permission is separate from player disclosure. The narrator may research later material privately while preserving the player's viewing boundary. Public files require the explicit local publication allowlist.
