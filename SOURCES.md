The full CEMK rulebook, Cyberpunk RED core book, World of Cyberpunk 2077 and regional sourcebooks were not supplied or read in full. Available publisher excerpts are sufficient for a clearly labelled original campaign baseline, but not for certifying every international detail or named implant as canon. Exact equipment models and destination-specific lore should receive focused checks before consequential use. No unofficial download of paid books is included.

## Spoiler policy

The effective boundary is the details already supplied in the private setup discussion. Search results can expose unwanted plot snippets, so do not store or repeat those snippets, use them for hidden campaign twists, or require the player to inspect a spoiler-rich citation. Advance this boundary only when the player says so. The public references contain no later-show facts.

## Later corrections

When new evidence arrives, record source, exact supported claim, era, previous campaign interpretation, proposed revision and effective event. A newly published official story does not silently erase player history. Maintain the alternate branch once play diverges. Keep future narrator plans out of public references and exact private records out of GitHub.
