# Sources and credits

The companion uses authored campaign conversions. These links identify its sources; they do not make the converted statistics official rules or supply future story information.

- [R. Talsorian Games downloads](https://rtalsoriangames.com/downloadable-content/): official Cyberpunk tabletop supplements and errata. Models and capacities can differ between editions.
- [Cyberpunk official site](https://www.cyberpunk.net/): the video-game setting and related media. Linked sites may contain spoilers.
- [D&D Systems Reference Document](https://www.dndbeyond.com/srd): the 2014 fifth-edition SRD 5.1 supports the rules foundation. SRD material is by Wizards of the Coast LLC, used under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), with campaign terminology and mechanics changed.
- The privately supplied 2014 Player’s Handbook and official errata informed the full local audit. The PDF and source extracts are not redistributed here. Additional PHB subclass material is adapted into campaign descriptions, distinct from the SRD licence.
- Magazine research distinguishes inspected publisher text, secondary model data and unresolved historical source notation. The narrator retains the detailed evidence ledger. Neither a conversion profile nor an internal check proves a canonical factory specification.

The character reference was supplied and approved for this companion by the player. Equipment illustrations, interface and RONIN emblem are campaign assets. They illustrate equipment rather than granting capabilities. Rajdhani is distributed under the [SIL Open Font License](docs/assets/OFL.txt).

The current character’s Cyclops Civic C2 and Rocklin Augmentics NP-1 model treatments are authored campaign equipment informed by the setting’s manufacturers and interface categories.
