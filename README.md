# Cyberpunk Stories campaign references

Reference material for a personal narrative Cyberpunk campaign beginning in Night City and capable of expanding worldwide. Setup is in progress; no opening scene or companion app has been published.

- [Economic reference](ECONOMY.md): living costs, income, gear, treatment, travel, property and business accounting. Verified historical anchors are distinguished from proposed campaign estimates.
- [Sources and continuity boundaries](SOURCES.md): inspected publisher material, era limitations and spoiler policy.

This is an unofficial personal campaign. Original campaign material is not official Cyberpunk canon. Copyrighted sourcebooks and artwork are not redistributed here.

The public repository contains only approved references. Exact transcripts, private character records, unrevealed plans and the narrator's century forecast stay outside it. A repository is not an app deployment; hosting and the phone companion remain to be established.
